2018 saw us return to The Netherlands once again. On top of our usual trip to [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/duinrell/), we arranged to spend a week in Bovenkarspel, in the region of West-Frisia, North Holland. After a successful crossing the year before, we took the overnight ferry again from Hull to Rotterdam.

Our Airbnb holiday home overlooked the harbour at Broekerhaven, just a few doors away from De Overhaal, a rather fantastic boat lift. We found Bovenkarspel and nearby [Enkhuizen]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/enkhuizen/) to be really lovely places, with plenty to see and do, like the fantastic Zuiderzee Museum and the wonderful city of Hoorn. From a cycling perspective, there isn't a huge amount to see, to be honest. While there's a few good cycle routes, it's mostly paint on road cycle lanes.

For the second week of our trip, we were back in Duinrell for our eighth visit, with five families again. We visited a few familiar places, going to the beach at [Wassenaarse Slag]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/wassenaarse-slag/), the miniature park at Madurodam and a few trips into [Wassenaar]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/wassenaar/). I even had chance to have a day by myself to explore, enjoying a ride to Zoetermeer.
