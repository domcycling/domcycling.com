Here's a collection of the many photos and videos I've taken over the years at Manchester Critical Mass, starting back in 2014.

For anyone that doesn't know, Critical Mass rides are a regular celebration of cycling that happen all round the world. [More about the Critical Mass movement and its history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_Mass_(cycling)).

In Manchester, Critical Mass happens on the last Friday of every month, meeting outside Manchester Central Library 6-6:30pm. There's no planned route and no leaders. Whoever is at the front decides where to go.

Join the [Manchester Critical Mass Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/ManchesterCriticalMass), for more details.
