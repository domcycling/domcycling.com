In 2015, we made our seventh trip to The Netherlands as a family, splitting the holiday between [The Hague]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/den-haag/) and [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/category/duinrell/).

We spent the first five days in an Airbnb in the heart of The Hague, a stone's throw away from the city hall, which gave us the chance to sample the city's extensive cycle infrastructure. The second week was at Duinrell, the fifth time we'd stayed there in four years.

In between, we rode to the beach at [Wassenaarse Slag]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/wassenaarse-slag/) and to the university city of [Leiden]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/leiden/), on a route that's a great example of Dutch bike infrastructure.
