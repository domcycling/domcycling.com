When the group Pavement announced they would be touring again in 2022, it gave my friend, who now lives in Helsinki, and I an excuse to meet up. So in November 2022, I travelled from Manchester to [Berlin]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/berlin/) and my friend from Helsinki, for an extended trip to see them in both Berlin and [Bremen]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/bremen/), plus a few days to catch up and drink a few beers.

After three days in Berlin, we caught the train to Bremen, changing at Hamburg, then headed back to Berlin for the final leg of the trip. This series covers all three stops. I later returned to Berlin with the family, which you can read about in [Berlin 23]({{ site.baseurl }}/series/berlin-23/).
