2019 saw us back in The Netherlands once again. On top of our usual visit to [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/duinrell/), we arranged a week in [Hulshorst]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/hulshorst/), in the province of Gelderland. We took the overnight ferry from Hull to Rotterdam again, taking our car with bikes on the back.

We stayed at the holiday park Droompark Bad Hoophuizen, overlooking the Veluwemeer, a long and narrow lake separating Gelderland and Flevoland. There isn't really much in Hulshorst or in the immediate area, though [Nunspeet]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/nunspeet/) and [Harderwijk]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/harderwijk/) are just short rides away. We also took the opportunity to return to [Utrecht]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/utrecht/), one of our favourite cities. Cycling in the area is generally pretty good, with decent quality cycling infrastructure along the main routes.

For the second week of our trip, we were back in Duinrell for our ninth visit, with five families in total again. We returned to some of the places we've been to many times before, including the beach at [Wassenaarse Slag]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/wassenaarse-slag/), the miniature park at Madurodam, [Leiden]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/leiden/) and a few trips into [Wassenaar]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/wassenaar/). I also took the opportunity to get a bit further afield with rides to [Voorburg]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/voorburg/) and [Delft]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/delft/).
