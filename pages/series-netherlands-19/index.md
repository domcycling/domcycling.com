2019 saw us back in The Netherlands once again. On top of our usual visit to [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/duinrell), we arranged a week in Hulshorst, in the province of Gelderland.

We took the overnight ferry from Hull to Rotterdam again, taking our car with bikes on the back. After years of doing the tunnel crossing, then driving through France and Belgium, we now prefer the more relaxed nature of the overnight ferry. Especially as it involves much less driving.

![20190525_154752](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190525_154752.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*On the ferry, waiting to leave Hull*

At some point, I'd love to do the journey, car-free. Though I'm having some trouble persuading the family on this. Maybe one day...

## Hulshorst

We arrived on the Sunday of the first week of our trip at the holiday park [Droompark Bad Hoophuizen,](https://www.droomparken.com/badhoophuizen) located in Hulshorst. In previous years, we've tended to book holiday homes through Airbnb, so this was a bit of a departure.

![20190528_200605](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190528_200605.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*The entrance to Droompark Bad Hoophuizen*

Hulshorst itself is a small village located between Harderwijk and Nunspeet. Harderwijk is a small city, located in the geographical centre of The Netherlands, about 50km east of Amsterdam.

The holiday park overlooked Veluwemeer, a long and narrow lake, separating Gelderland and Flevoland. The province of Flevoland is made up of reclaimed land and was originally part of the Zuiderzee, before it was drained. Last year, we stayed on the opposite side of the Zuiderzee in Enkhuizen, you can [read more about that trip here]({{ site.baseurl }}/2018/07/26/netherlands-18/).

![20190526_115952](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190526_115952.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*The beachfront bar, overlooking the Veluwemeer*

The weather for the first week was a little mixed. There was some lovely hot days and some slightly colder, overcast days. We managed to avoid any serious rain though, which was good. The holiday park had a lakeside beach with water sports and a modern, clean indoor pool. So there was things to do onsite, whatever the weather.

![20190526_201725](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190526_201725.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Riding through the holiday park, Droompark Bad Hoophuizen*

There isn't really much in Hulshorst or in the immediate area, though [Nunspeet]({{ site.baseurl }}/2019/07/27/netherlands-19-the-route-to-nunspeet/) and [Harderwijk]({{ site.baseurl }}/2019/07/31/netherlands-19-the-route-to-harderwijk/) are just short rides away. While staying at Hulshorst, we visited [Apenheul](https://apenheul.com/), a free-roaming zoo of primates. We also took opportunity to return to [Utrecht]({{ site.baseurl }}/2019/08/31/netherlands-19-utrecht-revisited/), one of our favourite cities.

![20190529_140848](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190529_140848.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Gorilla show at Apenheul*

Cycling in the area around Hulshorst is generally pretty good and there's decent quality cycling infrastructure along the main routes. The roads around the holiday park didn't have any cycling infrastructure to speak of. Mostly, this was fine as the roads were very quiet, though they did have quite high 60km/h speed limits.

![20190527_163018](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190527_163018.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Watch out on the agricultural roads around Hulshorst*

We really enjoyed our stay at [Droompark Bad Hoophuizen](https://www.droomparken.com/badhoophuizen). It was a lovely holiday park, with a great location over the lake. The accommodation was fantastic, really clean and modern, though the area we were in was a little quiet.

![20190526_141935](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190526_141935.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Very pleasant, modern accommodation at Droompark Bad Hoophuizen*

While the onsite restaurant was pretty good, it wasn't always open and was reserved for a wedding one evening. The onsite takeaway and shop were pretty poor. This seems to be a common theme of a few holiday parks we've stayed at.

## Duinrell

For the second week of our trip, we were back in Duinrell for our ninth visit. Like I've said before, we never intended to be the kind of people who stay at the same place every year, but it seems we are. Next year might be our last visit though.

![20190607_122950](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190607_122950.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Enjoying rides and candyfloss at Duinrell*

If you want to find out more about Duinrell, have a read of my post [Duinrell, a family and cycling friendly holiday park in The Netherlands]({{ site.baseurl }}/2017/04/16/duinrell-a-family-and-cycling-friendly-holiday-park-in-the-netherlands/), or some of [my other posts featuring Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/duinrell/).

We were lucky to get some great weather in our second week. We did experience a couple of crazy storms some evenings, but the daytime weather was fantastic. As usual, we planned our week around the weather, making the most of the sunshine by going to the beach and out for rides, then going to the theme park and pool when the weather wasn't so good.

![20190606_122630](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190606_122630.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Our gang, on the dune route to Madurodam*

There were five families in total again this year, all of whom had been before. We were spread across Duinrell a bit more than usual, though we still had opportunity to socialise together, choosing a different cabin as a base each night. Probably best, given I'd brought the ukuleles with us (when I'm not going on about cycling, I can often be found helping organise [Stretford Uke](https://stretforduke.wordpress.com/)).

![20190604_212715](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190604_212715.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Giving a few ukulele lessons on evening at Duinrell*

We returned to some of the places we've been to many times before, including the beach at Wassenaarse Slag, the miniature park at Madurodam, Leiden and a few trips into Wassenaar. In addition, I took the opportunity to get a bit further afield with rides to [Voorburg]({{ site.baseurl }}/2019/09/28/netherlands-19-the-route-to-voorburg/) and [Delft]({{ site.baseurl }}/2019/11/02/netherlands-19-the-route-to-delft/).

![20190604_141852](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190604_141852.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*One of the many gardens at Hortus Botanicus Leiden*

As I've said many times before, cycling around near Duinrell is so great, you don't need a car while you're there. There's so much that's in riding distance and the safe cycling infrastructure means cycling with the family is a pleasure, not a worry.

![20190606_170933](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/pages/series-netherlands-19/20190606_170933.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Stopping for a nosy at the Huis ten Bosch palace on the way back from Madurodam*

If you have kids and you're looking to experience what The Netherlands has to offer, both on and off the bike, then Duinrell is great option.
