After having a break in 2023, we returned to the Netherlands in 2024 for our eleventh visit as a family. As has become the custom in recent years, in addition to staying in Duinrell, we'd split the trip with another location in the Netherlands, ideally somewhere we've not been to.

The original plan was to stay in Rotterdam, which is somewhere we've visited, but not stayed at before. We did have an Airbnb booking arranged, but had to change it due to an increase in numbers, which couldn't be accommodated.

## First week in Amersfoort

So the first week of our Netherlands 24 trip saw us spending a week in the fabulous city of Amersfoort, staying in a lovely home, just round the corner from Amersfoort Centraal Station, with easy reach by bike or on foot and great cycling facilities.

During our week, we got to experience what Amersfoort has to offer, including lots of pleasant spaces to wander, great bars, restaurants, museums and cultural quarters. With some fantastic weather, we also got to check out a local outdoor pool in the forest.

With its proximity to Amersfoort, we also managed a couple of trips to the equally superb city of Utrecht. This gave me opportunity to check out the excellent F28 Fast Cycle Route between the two cities.

## Second week at Duinrell

After a wonderful week in Amersfoort, we made our way to Duinrell, meeting up with family for the second week of our trip. In addition to it being our eleventh visit to the Netherlands, it was also our eleventh visit to Duinrell.

Being old hands, we settled into a familiar routine, making use of the theme park rides and swimming pools on site, plus trips into Wassenaar and to the local beach at Wassenaarse Slag.

We also travelled further afield, with trips on our bikes to the nearby cities of Leiden and Den Haag, plus rides to more beaches at Scheveningen and Katwijk. I also managed to find time to head back to the amazing Burgemeester Waaijerbrug bridge and Voorburg via the Royal Palace 'Huis ten Bosch'.
