By 2016, The Netherlands was turning into a yearly visit for us. For the first week of the trip we stayed in [Vlissingen]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/vlissingen/), our first time in [Zeeland]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/zeeland/), having mainly stayed around North and South Holland before. While we were there, we took the bikes to [Middelburg]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/middelburg/), the capital of Zeeland.

We spent the second week at [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/category/duinrell/), our sixth visit, and finally made it to [Katwijk aan Zee]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/katwijk-aan-zee/).

As well as the places we went, this series includes a selection of photos of Dutch bikes and a look at some of the myths about cycling in The Netherlands, based on our experiences.
