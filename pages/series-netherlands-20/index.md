2020 was due to be our 10th consecutive trip to The Netherlands, with a stay at Vakantiepark Koningshof, not far from Katwijk, followed by a week at [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/category/duinrell/) with friends and family. Unfortunately, the coronavirus pandemic put a stop to that.

Rather than dwell on what might have been, I took the opportunity to look back at our previous trips, going back a long time before I started the blog. This series starts with my first time in The Netherlands, on a school trip to Maastricht in 1989, and goes on to our first time at Duinrell in 2012, our return in 2013, and our two trips in 2014, including a camping trip around Castricum and Duinrell.
