2022 saw us return to The Netherlands and [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/category/duinrell/) for the first time since 2019, after the pandemic put paid to our plans for 2020 and 2021. We arrived on the overnight Hull to Rotterdam ferry, with our usual haul of bikes on the back of the car.

We normally spend a week by ourselves before meeting up with friends and family at Duinrell, but this time we reversed it, spending the first week at Duinrell, followed by a week at an Airbnb in [Zaandam]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/zaandam/).

From Duinrell, we rode to Vlietland, [Wassenaarse Slag]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/wassenaarse-slag/), [Katwijk]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/katwijk-aan-zee/) and [Noordwijk]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/noordwijk-aan-zee/) and [Leiden]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/leiden/), and made a multimodal trip to [Rotterdam]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/rotterdam/). In Zaandam, we stayed in a lovely old Dutch house on a canal, which came with access to two boats. From there we explored NDSM, [Amsterdam]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/amsterdam/), Zaanse Schans and Hembrug, before a final day out in [Delft]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/delft/) on the way back to the ferry.
