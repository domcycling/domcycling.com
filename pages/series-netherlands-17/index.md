At the end of May 2017, we made what seems to be our annual visit to The Netherlands. On top of our usual trip to [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/duinrell/), we arranged to spend a week in [Utrecht]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/utrecht/) in an Airbnb apartment. For the first time, we took the overnight ferry from Hull to Rotterdam, which turned out to be a really enjoyable experience.

We found Utrecht to be a really lovely city and it lived up to everything we'd read about. Our apartment was 3km out of the centre on Amsterdamsestraatweg, a great location to get about, and the weather was pretty much glorious every day (except one day). There's lots of examples of great cycling infrastructure in and out of the city, whether you're looking for excellent cycleways or innovative cycle parking.

For the second week of our trip, we were back in Duinrell for our seventh visit. There were five families in total who knew each other, which meant for a very sociable holiday for both kids and grown ups. We made the most of the sunny days by getting to the beach and having days out, and spent the less great days in the theme park and pool.
