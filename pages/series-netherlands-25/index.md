In July 2025, we made our way to [Duinrell]({{ site.baseurl }}/category/duinrell/) for what would be our twelfth visit, meeting up with family, as we’d done in recent years. As before, we’d be staying in a [Eurocamp](https://www.eurocamp.co.uk/campsites/netherlands/south-holland/duinrell-campsite) holiday home, a Comfort XL and we’d be driving over with our bikes.

In a break from recent tradition, we'd arranged to spend the whole two weeks at Duinrell, rather than staying a week somewhere else in the Netherlands. Partly, this was as we knew the kids would enjoy that more, but also to give us more opportunity to explore nearby.

In addition to hanging around Duinrell, we ended up having multiple trips to the nearby cities of [Leiden]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/leiden/) and [Den Haag]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/den-haag/), as well as to [Delft]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/delft/), [Rotterdam]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/rotterdam/) and [Amsterdam]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/amsterdam/), with me cycling to all but the last one.

While we didn't get any proper beach weather during our trip, we did make visits to the local beaches at [Wassenaarse Slag]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/wassenaarse-slag/), [Katwijk aan Zee]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/katwijk-aan-zee/) and [Scheveningen]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/scheveningen/), which meant lots of riding through the dunes.
