The agenda for the next Trafford Cycle Forum has been distributed, details below.

6:00-8:00pm Tuesday 17th October 2017 – Trafford Town Hall

## Agenda

1. Welcome and Introductions
2. Actions [updates](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1beNKOKt1mS0B3pChic57FunmT70cfuxDNWNACq1x6qU/edit#gid=0) and approve [minutes from last meeting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14Bcfk7zvxLe6FaG4DDqW2_84ObGci07i15cq8P9VyqM)
3. Creating safe space for cycling
   * a. Sale town centre [consultation](http://www.investintrafford.com/TownCentres/Sale-Town-Centre-Consultation.aspx)
   * b. Potential opportunities from the [refreshed Stretford Masterplan](http://www.trafford.gov.uk/stretfordmasterplan)
   * c. A56 to Talbot Rd junction improvement
   * d. Amey / Trafford - key points from and since last technical meeting
4. Cycling strategies
   * a. GM Mayor’s office and Commissioner - update from Martin Key
   * b. Cycling Strategy/ies for Trafford in 17/18 and beyond
5. “Reasons to cycle” presentations from Manchester Cycle Forum
   * a. Tim - convenience
   * a. Nadia - safer, healthier streets
6. Cycling safety
   * a. A560 resurfacing [petition](https://democratic.trafford.gov.uk/mgEPetitionDisplay.aspx?ID=74&RPID=1267393&HPID=1267393) - see [Bob’s video](https://youtu.be/jNypVoI_zNc)
   * b. Revision of TCF’s position statement on cycle lane widths
   * c. Brooklands Road [petition](https://democratic.trafford.gov.uk/mgEPetitionDisplay.aspx?ID=79&RPID=0&HPID=0) - see [Bob’s video](https://youtu.be/fKZbxLRidag)
7. Open Forum / A.O.B. MN
8. Date and Location of Next Meeting(s)
   * a. Social meet-up: Nov 27 / 28, Dec 4 / 5 ? from 7.30pm at The Bridge Inn, Sale
   * b. Technical meeting: Pre-xmas, date TBC
   * c. 2018 Forums, 6-8pm at Trafford Town Hall: Tuesday 30th January, Tuesday 8th May
