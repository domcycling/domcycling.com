The agenda for the next Trafford Cycle Forum has been distributed, details below.

6:00-8:00pm Tuesday 25th July 2017 – Trafford Town Hall

## Agenda

1. Welcome and Introductions
2. Outstanding Actions and [minutes from last meeting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1W8ShqmagKgAlaa4b8BkCq-dqyDVVdhYih34BN00HN6U)
3. Trafford’s Member Cycling Champions
4. Update from Amey and the Technical Forum
   * a. Infrastructure update - [Stretford Cycleway plans](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/0B_1SI7iBXu3Bdmp4TFVQY0w5N2M)
   * b. Cycle route parking enforcement
   * c. Road maintenance schemes
5. Mobike public cycle hire scheme
6. Maps for forum members’ feedback
   * a. Reporting issues and opportunities: [guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1J0DZ-1BqIhSaVd4nYnL2konyX1VCcMU1XYMPPIwSGQ4), [demo](https://trafford.cyclestreets.net/photomap/tags/BarriersToCycling/)
   * b. Barriers on the Trans-Pennine Trail (and elsewhere?)
   * c. List of priority schemes
7. Trafford Plans for Cycling 17/18 and beyond
   * a. Trafford Scrutiny Report on Cycling ([link](https://democratic.trafford.gov.uk/documents/s10249/Cycling%20Review%20Scrutiny%20report%20Jan%202016.pdf), [notes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gchoh1ty_drP-OBSoFQ-oMlDPipafq6tHgRyl_sKNrs))
   * b. Trafford Cycling Strategy (TfGM [cycling strategy](http://cycling.tfgm.com/Pages/pdfs/Cycling-Strategy-summary.pdf), [2040 strategy](http://www.tfgm.com/2040/Pages/strategy/index.html))
   * c. Trafford’s Local Cycling and Walking Infrastructure Plan (DfT [CWIS](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cycling-and-walking-investment-strategy), [LCWIPs](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/cycling#local-cycling-and-walking-infrastructure-plans))
8. Open Forum / A.O.B.
9. Future Agenda Items
   * a. Metrolink Trafford Park Line - meeting to see updated plans
   * b. [CycleBOOM](http://cycleboom.org/) - Tim Jones, Oxford Brookes University
10. Date and Location of Next Meeting(s)
    * Social meet-up: Tuesday 19th September from 7.30pm at The Bridge Inn, Sale
    * Forums, all 6-8pm at Trafford Town Hall:
    * Tuesday 17th October 2017, Tuesday 30th January 2018, Tuesday 8th May 2018
