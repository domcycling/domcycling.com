title: Riding from Center Parcs Woburn Forest to Bletchley Park
---

After an [initial short ride outside Center Parcs Woburn Forest]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/05/31/short-ride-outside-center-parcs-woburn-forest/), during our Easter 2026 trip, I was keen to explore a bit further. With a trip planned to Bletchley Park, it seemed like a great opportunity for a ride.

[![YouTube video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/W-pw3KU-kIA/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W-pw3KU-kIA)
*Watch the full-length video [Riding from Center Parcs Woburn Forest to Bletchley Park](https://youtu.be/SjkMHPfUz8A)*

In planning the route, I relied on the [Cranfield Cycling](https://cranfieldcycling.co.uk/) blog, which was useful for the area near to Center Parcs. For the later sections of the ride, I relied on [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/) to provide a route, which was mostly successful.

Leaving Center Parcs, I set off on the route covered in the [Woburn Backroads ride](https://cranfieldcycling.co.uk/2024/11/17/woburn-backroads/). This started on the gravel path surrounding the Center Parcs perimeter fence that I covered in my last ride.

![On the path next to Center Parcs](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1458.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*On the path next to Center Parcs*

Continuing onto Rectory Road as I did on my last ride, I carried on straight and made my way to the M1 motorway. After passing under the motorway, I turned off onto the path across Wakes End Farm.

![Approaching M1 motorway bridge](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1467.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Approaching M1 motorway bridge*

The path across the farm was pretty bumpy, but was at least dry thanks to the recent weather. I was particularly bad near the paddocks, where the ground was covered in hoof prints, from when it was muddy.

![Bumpy path with dried mud through a field](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1479.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Bumpy path with dried mud through a field*

Leaving the farm, I turned right onto the C100 and made my way to Woburn Abbey Deer Park. Crossing the cattle grid, I made my way through the deer park, with a steady stream of cars passing me.

![Road through Woburn Abbey Deer Park](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1492.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Road through Woburn Abbey Deer Park*

Stopping briefly for photos of the deer and surroundings, I carried on through the deer park then exited through the gates. I then arrived in Woburn itself, a historic Georgian village in Bedfordshire with some fantastic old buildings.

![Zoohause building, Woburn](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1506.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Zoohause building, Woburn*

I turned right onto Newport Road and made my way out of the village, stopping to admire Staunton House. This includes a series of almshouses built for poor families in the nineteenth century.

![Staunton House, Woburn](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1512.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Staunton House, Woburn*

Once I'd made my way out of Woburn, I turned left onto another path next to a farmer's field. This to a series of old ruined buildings, that looked like they used to be old farm buildings.

![Old ruins](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1522.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Old ruins*

Turning right after the ruins, the path made its way up a fairly steep hill, with trees either side. At the top, the path flattened out as it made its may towards Aspley Woods. On the right was a small reservoir, with fencing around it. It not listed on any maps, so I'm not sure what it's for.

![Riding up the hill to the trees near Aspley Woods](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1526.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Riding up the hill to the trees near Aspley Woods*

I was now riding through the trees of Aspley Woods, where I eventually made my way onto Longslade Lane, where the woods continued on either side.

![Aspley Woods](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1529.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Aspley Woods*

Longslade Lane turned into Church Road as I continued through the Aspley Woods. Leaving the woods, I found myself at the top of the hill, with views looking out from Bow Brickhill all the way to [Milton Keynes]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/milton-keynes/) ahead.

![Looking out towards Milton Keynes from Church Road](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1545.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Looking out towards Milton Keynes from Church Road*

I made my way down the hill through Bow Brickhill, which was very enjoyable, as I continued onto Station Road. Turning right, I then passed Bow Brickhill station and over a level crossing, I now arrived on the outskirts of Milton Keynes, on V10 Brickhill Street.

![Level crossing in Caldecotte](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1548.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Level crossing in Caldecotte*

Turning off V10 Brickhill Street, I passed through some residential streets to arrive at Caldecotte Lake. Passing some Santander branded hire bikes, I made my way round the lake, on the path in front of a modern crescent, with a loose approximation of a Georgian style.

![Caldecotte Lake](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1555.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Caldecotte Lake*

The path round the lake eventually led up to the H10 Bletcham Way, where it ran next to the traffic lanes. Riding along, I passed round the far side of the lake, then over the River Ouzel.

![Bletcham Way, next to Caldecotte Lake](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1562.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Bletcham Way, next to Caldecotte Lake*

Continuing next to the H10 Bletcham Way, I now felt like I was back on one of Milton Keyne's [Redways]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/redways/). The Redway took me under the A5 on some very typical underpasses.

![Under the A5](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1573.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Under the A5*

Crossing over the [Grand Union Canal]({{ site.baseurl }}/tag/grand-union-canal/), there was a large space on the bridge between the path and the main carriageway, I'm guessing for a planned road that was nevery built. After crossing a couple of roundabouts, I arrived on the outskirts of Bletchley.

![Grand Union Canal](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1581.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Grand Union Canal*

I continued on the Redways as I made my way through Bletchley, riding parallel to the railway, then passing under a railway bridge. From there, it was a short ride along a few quiet streets, before arriving at Bletchley Park.

![Approaching railway bridge](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1586.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Approaching railway bridge*

I wasn't planning to go into Bletchley Park, though my other half and our youngest were already there, having driven over. I met up with them to collect our packed lunch, before heading back into Bletchley, to meet up with our eldest.

![Bletchley Park](https://f000.backblazeb2.com/file/domcycling/2026-07-12-riding-from-center-parcs-woburn-forest-to-bletchley-park/img_1592.webp){: loading="lazy"}
*Bletchley Park*
