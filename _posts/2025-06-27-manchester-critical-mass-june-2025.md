I wasn't able to make last month's Critical Mass, after going in [April]({{ site.baseurl }}/2025/04/25/manchester-critical-mass-april-2025/) for the first time in a long time. While it wasn't exactly sunny, it was pretty warm and remained dry for the ride, which is always a bonus!

[![YouTube video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/WqTdxAN9i60/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WqTdxAN9i60)

Given the weather wasn't exactly stunning, there was a pretty decent turnout for June. We also had a couple of sound systems along for the ride, which really make a difference.
