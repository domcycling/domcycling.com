The agenda for the next Trafford Cycle Forum has been distributed, details below.

6:00-8:00pm Tuesday 9th May 2017 – Trafford Town Hall

## Agenda

1. Welcome and Introductions
2. Outstanding Actions and minutes from last meeting
3. Maps for forum members’ feedback ([option 1](https://trafford.cyclestreets.net/photomap/tags/droppedkerb,absolutebollards/), [option 2](https://www.cyclescape.org/), [option 2 map](http://www.gmcc.org.uk/cyclescape-map/))
4. Update from Amey
   * Infrastructure Update
   * Cycle routes enforcement and maintenance
5. Trafford Plans for Cycling 17/18 and beyond
   * [DfT CWIS Publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cycling-and-walking-investment-strategy)
   * [Trafford Scrutiny Report on Cycling](https://democratic.trafford.gov.uk/documents/s10249/Cycling%20Review%20Scrutiny%20report%20Jan%202016.pdf)
   * Trafford Cycling Strategy
   * [An LCWIP for Trafford?](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/cycling#cycling-infrastructure)
6. Open Forum
   * Trafford's category for cycling and the forum - sport or transport?
7. Future Agenda Items
8. Dates of Next Meeting / Location
