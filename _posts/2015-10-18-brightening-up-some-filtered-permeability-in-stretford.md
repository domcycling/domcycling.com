title: Brightening up some filtered permeability in Stretford
---

Round the corner from Old Trafford football stadium on Railway Road. There's a little bit of filtered permeability that's just become a little brighter, courtesy of rather wonderful [Gorgeous Gorse Hill](http://gorgeousgorsehill.co.uk/) and local artist [Caroline Daly](https://www.etsy.com/shop/missdalys). As you can see below, the bollards feature bikes and bike components, in-line with their purpose.

The filtered permeability is part of a quiet route, running parallel to the A56 Chester Road. It connects up to Stretford town centre and [The Bridgewater Way](http://www.bridgewatercanal.co.uk/todo/bridgewater-way) near Watersmeet and emerges onto Matt Busby Way near Old Trafford football stadium. From there, it's possible to ride on a mostly traffic free route to Salford Quays and MediaCityUK, or cross over Chester Road and onto Talbot Road, which is a marginally better route into Manchester.
